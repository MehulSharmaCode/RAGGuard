Kestrel Ridge Observatory - Staff Handbook

Night access
The dome is open to staff between 19:00 and 05:00 from March to October.
- Visitors
  - must be accompanied by a staff member at all times
  - receive an orange badge at the gatehouse
- Students
  - may stay until 02:00 with a supervisor's written approval
  - receive a green badge
- Contractors
  - work only between 08:00 and 17:00
  - receive a grey badge and an escort

Equipment booking
The 1.2-metre telescope is booked through the scheduling board, and a booking holds for 48 hours if it is not confirmed. Spectrograph sessions need a calibration run before the first exposure, and the calibration lamp must be switched off after use because it overheats after 40 minutes.

Weather closures
- Wind above 45 km/h: close the dome immediately
- Humidity above 85%: keep the dome closed and cover the mirror
- Lightning within 10 km: shut down all instruments and leave the dome

Lost badges
Report a lost badge to the gatehouse on the same night. A replacement badge costs 15 euros and is issued the next working day.
