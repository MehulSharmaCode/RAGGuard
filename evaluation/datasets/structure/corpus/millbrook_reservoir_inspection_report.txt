Millbrook Reservoir - Annual Inspection Report 2025

The annual inspection of the Millbrook reservoir took place over three days in late April, when the water level was 2.1 metres below the spillway crest, which allowed the inspection team to examine the upstream face of the embankment along most of its length. The team walked the crest twice, measured settlement at the twelve survey pins installed in 2019, and compared the readings with the previous five annual reports; the maximum settlement since installation was 18 millimetres at pin 7, which remains within the design allowance of 40 millimetres. Seepage was measured at the three weirs at the downstream toe. The flow at weir B was 0.6 litres per second, slightly higher than in 2024 but clear and free of sediment, so it does not indicate internal erosion. The outlet tunnel was inspected by camera because the access ladder had been removed for repainting; the camera survey found minor calcite deposits but no cracking. Vegetation on the downstream face had grown above the permitted height in two areas, and the contractor was instructed to cut it before the end of June. The spillway gate was operated through its full range and closed in 3 minutes and 40 seconds, inside the 5-minute limit set by the operating manual. The emergency drawdown valve was not tested this year because the downstream channel was being dredged; it must be tested before 31 October 2025, and the dam safety engineer will attend that test. The overall condition of the reservoir is rated satisfactory, and the next full inspection is due in April 2026.
