Harlow Institute - Parking Permits, South Campus

Annual permit
The annual permit for the South Campus costs 310 euros and is valid from 1 January to 31 December. It covers car parks S1 and S2.

Evening permit
Evening permits cost 75 euros per term and allow parking after 18:00 in car parks S1 and S2.

Electric vehicles
The South Campus has 6 charging points in car park S2. Charging costs 0.25 euros per kWh for everyone.

Lost permits
A replacement South Campus permit costs 35 euros from the security desk.
