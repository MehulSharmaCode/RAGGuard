Harlow Institute - Parking Permits, North Campus

Annual permit
The annual permit for the North Campus costs 240 euros and is valid from 1 September to 31 August. It covers car parks N1 to N4.

Evening permit
Evening permits cost 60 euros per term and allow parking after 17:30 in car park N2 only.

Electric vehicles
The North Campus has 14 charging points in car park N3. Charging is free for permit holders for up to 4 hours.

Lost permits
A replacement North Campus permit costs 20 euros from the estates office.
