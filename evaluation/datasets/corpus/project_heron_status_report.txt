Project Heron - Status Report, Q2 2025
Prepared by the Larkspur Hydrographics programme office.

Summary
Project Heron is a coastal monitoring network for the Vellmar Estuary, commissioned by the Vellmar Water Authority. The network uses TideWatch T3 buoys to track dissolved oxygen and salinity near the estuary mouth.

Progress
By the end of June 2025, 9 of the planned 14 buoys had been deployed. The remaining 5 buoys are scheduled for deployment in September 2025, after the summer fishing season.

Budget
The approved project budget is 480,000 euros. Spending to date is 291,000 euros, which is within plan.

Risks
The main risk is vandalism: two buoys were damaged by boats in May 2025 and were repaired under the project's insurance policy. A second risk is the delayed delivery of solar panels from the supplier.

Team
The project manager is Ingrid Solberg. The lead field engineer is Tomas Oyelaran.

Next milestone
The next milestone is the interim data review with the Vellmar Water Authority on 15 October 2025.
