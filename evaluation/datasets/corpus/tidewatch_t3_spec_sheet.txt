Larkspur Hydrographics - TideWatch T3 Product Specification Sheet
Document version 2.1, issued March 2025.

Overview
The TideWatch T3 is a moored water-quality sensor buoy designed for coastal and estuarine monitoring. It measures water temperature, salinity, dissolved oxygen, and turbidity, and transmits its readings to the Larkspur Cloud dashboard.

Physical characteristics
The buoy hull is 62 centimetres in diameter and weighs 18.5 kilograms without the mooring chain. The hull is made of UV-stabilised polyethylene and is painted signal yellow. The sensor cage is made of marine-grade 316 stainless steel.

Operating limits
The submerged sensor head is rated for continuous operation at depths of up to 300 metres. The operating water temperature range is -2 to 35 degrees Celsius. The buoy should not be deployed in locations where ice cover is expected to last longer than 14 days.

Power and battery
The T3 uses a 12-volt lithium iron phosphate battery pack with a capacity of 40 ampere-hours. With the default sampling interval, a fully charged pack lasts approximately 21 days without solar input. The optional 20-watt solar panel extends autonomy indefinitely in most temperate locations.

Sampling and communication
The default sampling interval is 10 minutes and can be configured between 1 minute and 6 hours. Readings are transmitted over LTE-M. Where no cellular coverage is available, the Iridium satellite module (sold separately) can be used instead. Data is buffered on the buoy for up to 90 days if transmission fails.

Calibration and maintenance
The dissolved-oxygen sensor must be recalibrated every 60 days. The anti-fouling wiper on the sensor head should be replaced every 6 months. Larkspur recommends a full service inspection once per year.

Warranty
The TideWatch T3 carries a 3-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects. Damage caused by collisions with vessels or by improper mooring is not covered by the warranty.
