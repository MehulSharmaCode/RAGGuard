Larkspur Hydrographics - Travel and Expense Policy
Effective 1 April 2025. This policy replaces and supersedes the Travel and Expense Policy of 1 January 2023.

Scope
This policy applies to all employees and contractors travelling on company business.

Booking
Flights and hotels must be booked through the company travel portal at least 21 days before departure where possible. Economy class is required for all flights shorter than 8 hours. Rail travel is preferred for journeys under 500 kilometres.

Meal allowance
Employees receive a daily meal allowance of 60 euros for domestic travel and 85 euros for international travel. Receipts are not required for the meal allowance.

Accommodation
The nightly hotel limit is 150 euros in most cities and 210 euros in capital cities.

Mileage
Use of a private car for business travel is reimbursed at 0.35 euros per kilometre.

Expense claims
Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the end of the trip through the expenses app. Approval by the employee's line manager is required for every claim. Claims submitted after 30 days require written approval from the finance director.

What changed from 2023
The main changes are higher meal allowances and hotel limits, a shorter submission deadline for expense claims, and the preference for rail travel.
