Larkspur Hydrographics - Travel and Expense Policy
Effective 1 January 2023.

Scope
This policy applies to all employees travelling on company business.

Booking
Flights and hotels must be booked through the company travel portal at least 14 days before departure where possible. Economy class is required for all flights shorter than 6 hours.

Meal allowance
Employees receive a daily meal allowance of 45 euros for domestic travel and 65 euros for international travel. Receipts are not required for the meal allowance.

Accommodation
The nightly hotel limit is 130 euros in most cities and 180 euros in capital cities.

Mileage
Use of a private car for business travel is reimbursed at 0.30 euros per kilometre.

Expense claims
Expense claims must be submitted within 60 days of the end of the trip. Claims submitted later than this will not be reimbursed.
