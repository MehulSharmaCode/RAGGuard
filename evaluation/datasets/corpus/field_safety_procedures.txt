Larkspur Hydrographics - Field Safety Procedures for Boat Operations
Issued by the Health and Safety Office.

Crew requirements
Every boat operation requires a minimum crew of two people. At least one crew member must hold a valid powerboat licence and a first-aid certificate.

Personal protective equipment
Life jackets must be worn at all times on deck. Steel-toed boots and cut-resistant gloves are required when handling mooring chains.

Weather limits
Boats must not be launched when the sustained wind speed exceeds 25 knots or when the forecast wave height exceeds 1.5 metres. The skipper has final authority to cancel or end a trip because of the weather.

Communication
The crew must file a trip plan with the shore station before departure and check in by VHF radio every 2 hours. If a scheduled check-in is missed by more than 30 minutes, the shore station starts the emergency procedure.

Equipment checks
The first-aid kit and the fire extinguisher on each boat must be inspected monthly. Flares must be replaced before their expiry date.

Incidents
All incidents and near misses must be reported to the Health and Safety Office within 24 hours using the incident form.
